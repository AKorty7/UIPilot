Licence:   SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1
           Full text: Art/Fonts/Michroma-OFL.txt, and https://openfontlicense.org

The font is used for the lettering of the menus UIPilot generates. Under the OFL you may
use it in your own games, commercial or not, and redistribute it with them. You may not
sell the font by itself. If you modify the font, the modified version must stay under the
OFL and may not use the name "Michroma".

To use a different typeface: select any generated text object and change
TextMeshPro > Font Asset. UIPilot does not depend on Michroma being present; if the font
asset is removed, newly generated menus fall back to TextMeshPro's default font.
